---
title: a link with long open edges no longer freezes the STL and 3MF writers
bump: patch
---
### Fixed

- Before writing an STL or 3MF, `stl()`, `threemf()` and `mesh()` look along each open edge for loose corners to mend it at. They searched every millimetre cell of the edge's box, so the work grew with the cube of a slanted edge's length: a link of 46 characters holding one open triangle 400 mm across took about 10 seconds to write, and one a metre across took minutes, locking the viewer's tab (a link ending `&get=stl` starts the download as the page opens). Each open edge now takes the cheapest of three searches: its box (a short or square-on edge), only the cells its line passes through (a long slanted edge, so the work grows with its length), or a look at each loose corner in turn (when there are few). The 400 mm triangle now writes in a few milliseconds.
- The searches also share a work budget sized from the mesh (two million cells or corners looked at, plus a hundred for each triangle). A mesh made to be slow, such as thousands of long open triangles side by side, used up to minutes even with the faster searches; once the budget is spent, the edges left are written as they are, not mended, so the writers finish in a second or two.
- The budget counts the loose corners met in each cell as well as the cells, so thousands of tiny open triangles packed into one millimetre cell (19 seconds for 6,000 of them) no longer get round it. The parts of one file share one budget, so a file of many such parts cannot spend a budget for each. `mesh()` takes the shared budget as an optional second argument, `{ budget }`; called with one argument it works as before.
- A link whose parts together name more triangles than a link can hold (400,000; parts may share triangles, so a few dozen characters of parts list could make the writers write and mend the model hundreds of times over), or that lists more parts than an assembly can have places (2,048), is refused as "model too big for a link". Every model rulecad makes is well inside both: the archive's largest has 250 parts.
- Nothing to do when upgrading: every part mends to exactly the same triangles as before (checked on all 1,021 parts of the 64 models in the public archive, none of which comes near the budget). No printed size changes.
