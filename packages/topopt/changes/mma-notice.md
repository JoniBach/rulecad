### Added

- A note, THIRD-PARTY.md, shipped with the package: `mma`, `subsolve` and `gauss` in topopt.mjs follow Krister Svanberg's MATLAB `mmasub.m` and `subsolv.m`, Copyright (C) 2006-2009 Krister Svanberg, which are under the GNU General Public License version 3 or later. The rest of the package is MIT. The three functions are being rewritten from his published papers; until then, treat them as possibly under the GPL if you reuse them. The README gains a Licence section saying the same. The code is unchanged: nothing a design builds, and no printed size, changes.
