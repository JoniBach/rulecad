# Changelog: @rulecad/viewer

What changed in each release, for someone upgrading a workspace.

## 0.3.13: parts in hand on the build page

### Changed

- A build page's bought part in hand (`"have": true` in the assembly) says so: `nema17 (17HS4401), in hand`.

## 0.3.12: a frame's sheets (Frames 3)

### Added

- A link's cut-and-drill sheets (`Beams: A`, `Beams: B` ..) open in a Beams tab, as a board's views do, and a beam
  picked in the model (labelled by its mark, `mark A: ...`) offers its own sheet with a `Sheet A ›` button.

## 0.3.11: a frame's build

### Added

- The build page's "Building the frame" section: each numbered step is a button that shows the model at that step.
